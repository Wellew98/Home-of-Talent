# Home of Talent — Photo Shot List

For the owner. Goal: fill the six service sections that currently have no real
photos, and start building genuine before/after pairs.

Everything here is shootable on a phone. No camera, no photographer.

---

## PART 1 — Paste this into WhatsApp

> Hi — for the website I need photos of the work. All on your phone, nothing fancy.
> Three things that make the biggest difference:
>
> *1. Send them as a Document, not a Photo.*
> Attach → Document → Browse → pick the photos. WhatsApp shrinks normal photos and
> they come out blurry on a big screen. As a Document they arrive full size.
>
> *2. Before and after, from the same spot.*
> Before you start a job, stand somewhere and take one photo. When you finish, stand
> in the *exact same spot*, same height, and take it again. Same angle, same distance.
> Those two side by side are the most powerful thing on the website.
>
> *3. Tidy up first, then shoot.*
> Pack the tools away, take out the packaging and offcuts, open the curtains and put
> the lights on. Two minutes of tidying is worth more than any editing.
>
> Per job I only need about 6 photos:
> • One wide shot of the whole finished space (stand in the doorway, shoot across the
>   room diagonally — it makes rooms look bigger)
> • Two closer shots of the work itself
> • One close-up of the detail you're proudest of
> • One of the team actually working — hands on tools
> • The before shot from the same spot
>
> Turn the phone sideways (landscape) for the wide ones.
> Don't photograph the client's personal things — family photos, post, documents.
> Ask before you photograph anyone's face.
>
> Thanks — send them whenever, no rush.

## PART 2 — What each website section needs

Six services have no real photo at all. These are the priority.

### 1. Interior Remodeling — EMPTY
- Finished room, wide, shot from a doorway corner diagonally across the space
- Work in progress: a wall opened up, new bulkhead, ceiling or built-in going in
- Close-up of a finish — skirting, cornice, a clean joint

### 2. Kitchen & Bathroom — EMPTY
- Finished kitchen, wide, from the doorway. Counters clear.
- Tiled shower or bath, shot straight-on
- Close-up: new countertop edge, tap, or cabinet handle

### 3. Painting & Finishes — EMPTY
- Hand and brush cutting in along a ceiling line (this is the money shot)
- Roller on a wall, mid-stroke
- Finished painted room, wide, natural light

### 4. Garage & Storage — EMPTY
- Fitted shelving or built-in storage, finished
- Garage door or garage interior, tidy
- Close-up of the joinery or bracket work

### 5. Garden & Outdoor Living — EMPTY
- Braai area, patio or paving, finished — shoot late afternoon, the light is better
- Boundary wall, deck or pergola
- Wide shot showing the space in use, no people needed

### 6. Repairs & Punch Lists — EMPTY
- Hands fixing something small: door hinge, tap, latch, cracked wall being filled
- The crack/damage before, the repair after — same spot
- A toolbag or tools laid out on a clean surface

### 7. Exterior Maintenance — THIN
Have roof work only. Also need: gutter and fascia repair, exterior wall repaint,
a finished exterior wide shot.

### 8. Seasonal Maintenance — THIN
Have the underlay/sisalation shots. Also need: gutter clearing, waterproofing being
applied, storm damage repair.

### 9. Smart Home & Water Monitoring — THIN
Have one pipe trench. Also need: a fitted device on a wall, water meter or pump
install, tank or geyser work, anything with a display or sensor on it.

## PART 4 — Why "send as Document" matters

Current photos arrive at 1280×720 to 1600×1200 because WhatsApp compresses them.
The phone originals are 3–4000px. On a full-width hero at desktop size, a 1280px
photo has to be stretched about 15% and starts to look soft.

Attach → Document → Browse → select photos. Same effort, four times the resolution.

---

## PART 5 — Quick reference: the six rules

1. Send as **Document**, not Photo
2. **Tidy the space** before shooting — biggest single quality lever
3. **Light behind you**, never shoot into a window
4. **Landscape** (phone sideways) for anything wide
5. **Same spot, same height** for before/after
6. **Six photos per job**, not forty — wide, two mediums, one detail, one working, one before
